Support

Need help? Email support@clarkestudios.com and include your browser or device and what you were trying to do.

Common questions

How do I steer? On desktop use WASD or arrow keys. On a phone use the on-screen stick. Device tilt is not wired in the web demo yet.

The game is blank or stuck. Try a current Chrome, Safari, or Firefox build with WebGL enabled. Open the full-screen demo if the embedded frame is clipped.

Where is my progress? In this browser’s local storage only. Clearing site data resets medals and unlocks.

Sound? Press M to mute/unmute. Some browsers block audio until you interact with the page.
