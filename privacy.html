Privacy

Last updated: November 2026.

What we collect

The Starfruit Orbit web demo does not collect personal data. Progress and settings stay in your browser’s local storage on your device. There are no accounts, analytics SDKs, or ad trackers in this build.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email, contact list, location, photos, or advertising identifier. There is no server backend for the web demo.

Future iOS build

If an App Store version ships later, this page will be updated before submission. Motion sensor data would be used on-device only to steer the ball. In-app purchases, if any, would be processed by Apple.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.
